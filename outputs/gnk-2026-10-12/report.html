Read the two valuation lenses together

The base scenario places the share price below estimated fleet asset NAV and above the value supported by modeled remaining-life cash flows. This is a conditional research result. The gap does not establish mispricing: asset marks, the cash bridge, future rates and fleet renewal can change the conclusion.

Conditional valuation: aggregate broker fleet mark is allocated by carrying value; post-quarter cash is estimated. Historical forecast validation is not included in this release.

Cash flows end when the fleet retires

The forecast starts the day after valuation, models twelve quarters, then runs annually through retirement. Delivery payments, maintenance, drydocking, working capital and scrap proceeds are explicit. No perpetual terminal value or unfunded replacement fleet is assumed.

Base next-year unlevered free cash flow is $142.7m. Cash after assumed debt repayments and interest is $120.9m, before dividends. It is not a dividend forecast.

What does the market price require?

Under the fixed base assumptions, the reverse valuation requires 16.1% higher open charter rates relative to the base vector. The Python solution is repriced through the full model, including the extra working capital associated with higher revenues.

Evidence and remaining research

  • The $1.42bn June fleet value is inferred from management’s rounded 18% net LTV disclosure; per-vessel values are allocated by carrying value, not individual broker marks.
  • Genco Volunteer is added at its disclosed purchase price less the disclosed delivery-delay adjustment, rather than a new October broker appraisal.
  • The July–October operating cash bridge is estimated; the $50m July revolver draw and $0.80 August dividend are explicit.
  • Open-fleet scenarios do not reconstruct every vessel charter or derivative position.
  • The debt facility is modeled as a 2031 bullet; this is a liquidity schedule, not a covenant or refinancing forecast.
  • Drydock cash is normalized from management budgets rather than a vessel-by-vessel survey calendar.
  • No replacement fleet is assumed after existing vessels retire; scrap proceeds use a DWT proxy.
